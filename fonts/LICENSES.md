# Self-hosted fonts

The fonts are the same files Google Fonts served on 2026-10-21. `fonts.css` is the css2 response for
`Syne:wght@400;600;700;800`, `DM+Sans:wght@300;400;500;600` and `JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500` with `display=swap`, with the URLs rewritten to `/fonts/`.
Its `@font-face` rules are also inlined in `index.html`, between the `SP-FONTS` markers.

All three families use the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows self-hosting and redistribution as long as the license travels with the fonts:
- Syne: `OFL-syne.txt`
- DM Sans: `OFL-dmsans.txt`
- JetBrains Mono: `OFL-jetbrainsmono.txt`

The license texts come from https://github.com/google/fonts (`ofl/<family>/OFL.txt`).
